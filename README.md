# GuildDB

Roster profile specs use a dropdown with your class's three talent trees. GuildDB detects and shares your main spec from the tree with the most spent talent points, refreshing when talents change. A manual dropdown choice lasts until your talent allocation changes. Tied, unspent or unavailable talent data does not force a main spec. Other members running the updated addon share their own detected specs; offline or non-addon members retain their last saved profile.

Use `/gd spec` to print your detected main spec, the points spent in each talent tree, and your saved profile spec (including any active manual override). This diagnostic only prints locally; it does not change or share your profile.

GuildDB is a World of Warcraft guild addon for a shared roster, member profiles, announcements, raids, events, shoutouts, and guild utilities. It is designed to work independently for any guild.

## Features

- DKP is a guild-wide opt-in feature under Settings > Officer > DKP (testing). Its single tab contains level-60 Standings, History, Auctions, Loot, Raids and Officer tools. It includes themed auction popups, MS/OS bids, loot charges, shared main/alt balances, starting grants, recorded raid awards, audited reversals and backup/restore. During testing, write access belongs to the GM and rank-1 officers; regular members can bid, inspect prices/history and control their own auction popups. See the DKP workflow below.

- Guild Achievements replaces Activity, preserving its guild-wide visibility setting. Achievement cards use gold accents for raid clears, member class colors for level milestones (theme accent when class data is unavailable), matching level icons, and profession-specific icons shared with milestone popups. Both Achievements and Recent Activity cards have matching tinted backgrounds, colored titles, and colored sidebars/borders. Achievement cards celebrate levels 10/20/30/40/50/60 and profession skill milestones at 150/225/300, with filters for levels, professions, and raid clears. Officers (GM or rank 1) can use Record raid clear to add a named clear and details; clears are labeled as officer-recorded, without claiming an automatic guild first. Retained activity seeds awards on upgrade; discarded history and unreported accomplishments cannot be reconstructed. Awards retain the latest 500 without a time expiry; history sync shares the latest 100. Only the guild master (rank 0) can Delete an individual award or Clear category (including all listed raid clears), with confirmation. Incoming and relayed deletions must also be authored by the guild master; rank 1 can still record raid clears. Deletions sync and suppress old or re-imported milestone records so cleared awards stay removed; recent activity remains separate. Achievements, Recent Activity, and Shoutouts are separate views, with Achievements selected initially. Each view keeps its own category filter while switching; switching views resets the scroll position. Raid-clear recording and GM-only award deletion appear in Achievements; GM/rank 1 shoutout deletion appears in Shoutouts. Recent Activity shows all retained reports, with All activity, Levels and Professions filters, showing level 10/20/30/40/50/60 and profession milestones only, backed by the existing 200-entry/30-day feed and 50-entry activity sync. Popup previews and tests are excluded, and popup preferences do not disable tracking. Real milestones broadcast durable activity records independently of popup sender-name matching. After login or a guild change, each character re-shares their retained personal milestone awards once so missed achievements can catch up; no awards are invented from current levels.
- Recruitment is visible only to the GM and rank 1 officers, matching GuildDB's officer permissions. Open it with `/gd recruit`. Search Who or import the game's current Who results to save unguilded candidates, or add a full character name manually. Search saved candidates and choose a status from the themed dropdown to review contacts, replies, skipped candidates, and Do Not Contact entries. Whisper and Guild invite each require a click; invites also require the game's guild-invite permission. Contact statuses indicate submitted actions, not confirmed delivery or membership.
- Recruitment includes private notes and saved whisper/ad templates with `{name}` and `{guild}` placeholders. Save templates before sending. Post ad uses the joined channel number you enter and has a five-minute cooldown. Candidate data, latest replies from previously contacted players, templates, and cooldowns are saved locally per recruiter and guild; they are not sent through guild sync. Other officers maintain their own lists. Recruitment ads, whispers and guild invites can be submitted in combat. Contacts have a two-second shared cooldown.
- Recruitment separates Candidates and Messages into two pages, with bordered work areas for finding players, the candidate list, selected-player actions and notes, whisper templates, advertisements, and contact limits. Its boxes, inputs, labels, and selected rows follow the active theme and accent. Workspace scrollbars share a compact accent-colored thumb, support dragging and the mouse wheel, and hide when their content fits.
- Recruitment has a draggable compact window: click Pop out in Recruitment or use `/gd recruit popout`. Drag its bottom-right grip to resize; the preview, replies and controls expand with it. It follows the selected theme and accent color. It works while the main window is closed, with Who search, one list dropdown for New, Contacted, Replied, Invited, Skipped, Do Not Contact, All saved and Import Who results, a scrollable candidate dropdown with class colors and contact status, expanded whisper preview, latest replies, Whisper, Guild invite, Skip / Next, Do Not Contact and Restore. Full window returns to the main workspace with the current candidate/search. Its size and position are saved privately per recruiter and guild. Existing contact limits, exclusions and click requirements still apply; it closes on loss of recruiter permission, a guild change, or disabling Recruitment.
- Recruitment Who searches replace the visible results instead of accumulating previous searches. A single level such as `22` matches that level exactly; inclusive ranges such as `1-60` or `50-60` exclude levels outside the range and unreadable levels. Other Who filters use the latest results returned by the game. Editing the Who filter hides the previous results until you run the new search; waiting, empty, and timed-out searches never show the older list. Saved list returns to all saved candidates, with the status dropdown and saved-candidate text search still applied. Contact history, skips, Do Not Contact, and cooldowns are preserved when switching searches.
- Recruitment opens with an empty candidate workspace, including after a reload or returning from another tab. Run Search Who or Import results to show search results, or explicitly choose Saved list to browse retained candidates. Closing the workspace clears only its displayed view (handing off to the compact window preserves the active search); saved history, exclusions, notes, and cooldowns remain intact. Late Who replies from a closed workspace do not reopen the previous results.
- Home's Message of the day, Announcements, and Recently joined boxes each use the shared compact scrollbar, including theme/accent updates. Input-form dropdown lists use that same scrollbar with space reserved beside the options.
- The profile editor and other input forms use the same themed X close button as the main window, including updates when the appearance changes.
- Skip blocks contact for one hour; Do Not Contact blocks whispers and invites until Restore is explicitly clicked. Restoring or reimporting a candidate never clears contact cooldowns. There is a ten-second delay between contact actions, a 24-hour cooldown per candidate for each action (whisper and invite separately), and a daily limit of 50 actions per recruiter, resetting on the client's local date. Limits survive reloads. Who searches have a ten-second delay, and clients without readable Who APIs can still add candidates manually. No messages or invitations are sent automatically.
- Online-only guild roster with GuildDB presence and reported addon version.
- Guild-wide tab settings have a separate lightweight sync on login, guild changes, opening GuildDB, and manual re-sync, with startup retries. Any guild peer with a saved officer-authored snapshot can relay it when the GM is offline. Snapshots received before roster names and ranks are ready are retried after roster updates, including offline officers. First-time non-officer installs keep Home, Roster, and Settings available while waiting instead of enabling every optional tab; the saved guild selection applies as soon as it arrives. Settings → Personal shows whether guild tab settings are loaded. Personal theme, scale, and other preferences remain local.
- Roster filters combine class, role (including Unknown), level range, and profession with the existing search and Online Only setting. Clear filters resets the four filters while preserving search and Online Only. The result count includes characters; role and profession rely on shared profiles. Filters stay active while switching tabs during the current session and are not sent to guild peers.
- The top-left header icon displays the guild tabard emblem with its native background and border colors. It refreshes with the window and guild/tabard events, using the faction icon while guild data or tabard rendering is unavailable.
- Minimap launcher: enabled by default and registered as GuildDB through bundled LibDataBroker/LibDBIcon libraries, including in minimap-button menus. Left-click toggles GuildDB, right-click opens Settings, and dragging repositions it around the minimap. Its position survives reloads. Show or hide it under Settings → Personal → Show minimap button; `/gd` still works when the button is hidden.
- Opt-in guild member location sharing with live World Map pins. Enable sharing in Personal Settings; no separate Map tab is needed.
- Member profiles for role, specialization, time zone, alts, and listed professions or recipes.
- Home dashboard above Roster with a message of the day, officer announcements, and recently observed new members.
- The guild master and officers can always edit the MOTD. Home's Save button submits the game's MOTD command directly from your click and requires the game's Edit MOTD permission. Home displays the current guild MOTD, including changes made through the game's guild window.
- Recently joined tracks members from the 0.1.88 release onward, using a shared release cutoff. Earlier joins are excluded automatically. Names show class colors and levels. Live notifications and available guild history supply joins; guild-log entries too imprecise to place after the cutoff are excluded. Records are retained for 30 days.
- Analytics shows guild makeup with class, race, spec, role, and level charts. Switch between all roster members and online members; hover a chart row for its count and percentage. Race, spec, and role use shared profiles, with missing values counted as Unknown. Counts represent characters, including alts.
- GuildDB can open, close, refresh, and switch tabs during combat. Protected guild-rank actions stay hidden until combat ends.
- Guild Info has separate Guild Info and Guild Rules pages, editable by the GM and rank 1 officers. Empty installations include starter guild rules and an introduction explaining that the guild is growing and GuildDB is a work in progress. Existing saved information is preserved; edits are shared with guild peers. Each page keeps its own unread status.
- The guild master can save or remove a Discord server invite under Settings → GM. A Join Guild Discord button using the shared button style and selected accent color stays in the window header on every tab and is disabled until an invite is configured. It opens a selected, copyable link with Ctrl+C instructions. Invites sync with guild data; only rank 0 can set them.
- Raid creation and signup area with raid size targets, player specs, Tank/Healer/DPS roles, attendance, and signup notes. The GM can check players for the invite roster; selections are shared with the guild.
- PvP leaderboard with outdoor kills, deaths, K/D ratios, seasons, and a Most Wanted list. K/D is kills divided by deaths, using one as the denominator until the first death. Shared honorable kills count as kills; assists are not tracked separately.
- PvP includes Leaderboard, Bounty Board, and Nemesis views. Bounties open automatically when an enemy has killed guild members three times, including repeat kills of the same member. Three deaths gives +3 bonus points, five gives +5, and the reward caps at +10. No officer creates, approves, or edits rewards. The first subsequent credited outdoor kill freezes the reward; every guild member receiving kill credit in that defeat's 10-second credit window gets the full bonus once. The enemy's death counter restarts after a claim; new deaths build the next bounty. There is no daily payout cap or expiry. Bounties span seasons; each member's bonus counts toward the season containing their credited kill and all-time points.
- The leaderboard ranks by total points (kills + bounty bonuses), showing points separately from kills, deaths, and K/D. Nemesis shows your kills and deaths against each enemy, sorted by Most killed or Killed me most, for the selected season or All-time. Bounty Board shows the current guild-wide counter, bonus and status: OPEN, CLAIM (collecting shared credit), or PAID. Enemies with only one or two guild kills are tracked internally and hidden until the third opens a bounty; fresh counters after a payout also stay hidden until they reach three.
- Bounty combat evidence is saved separately from the capped activity feed and synced for consistent awards after reload, history trimming and out-of-order delivery. Retained events seed the initial history, but discarded encounters cannot be reconstructed from cumulative counters. All participants should update for complete bounty evidence and matching leaderboards. Shared defeats are grouped by enemy name and event timestamps; the client does not provide a shared kill-credit ID. Deleting a season keeps all-time bonuses; Wipe All Stats also clears bounty evidence and rejects stale pre-reset packets.
- Updated members save and sync cumulative enemy totals beyond the recent-history cap. Retained history seeds personal totals on upgrade; discarded encounters cannot be recovered. Members on older versions contribute retained encounters only. Full names including realms remain distinct. Deleting a season removes its rivalry totals; Wipe All Stats also clears rivalries.
- Outdoor PvP deaths count once per life when you took player or player-controlled pet damage in the preceding 30 seconds. Death records and season/all-time totals sync automatically with guild peers. Detection requires readable combat-log damage events from the client.
- Upcoming raid/event cards highlight SIGNUP NEEDED until you respond Going, Maybe, or Absent; locked signups direct you to an officer. New signup forms remember the last successfully saved role and spec per character and realm across reloads, with existing event choices taking precedence. Attendance and notes are not copied between events. Roster right-click includes Copy character name, opening the full name selected for Ctrl+C.
- Personal Settings includes 15-minute raid/event reminders, enabled by default, for Going and Maybe signups. While logged in, GuildDB reminds you once per scheduled start in chat and, when popups are enabled, a clickable popup opening Raid Info. Logging in or signing up inside the final 15 minutes triggers the reminder on the next check (within 10 seconds). Receipts are saved per character and guild across reloads; rescheduling permits a new reminder. Absent, removed, deleted, past, and preview events are skipped. These reminders and receipts are never synced to guild peers.
- Requests combines Looking For and Crafting in separate scrollable panels with their own posting forms. Group posts expire after three hours; crafting requests expire after seven days and can be claimed by crafters.
- Officer polls appear as individual cards with colored horizontal bars, option names and vote totals above each bar, and a Vote button beneath it. Bar lengths show each option's share of the votes, with percentages beside the totals. Use the buttons to vote or change your choice; your selection is marked Voted. Each poll has separate Close and Delete controls for the GM and rank 1 officers. Closing preserves that poll's results; deleting removes it and syncs the deletion so old history cannot restore it. Voter names are not shown in the interface.
- Shoutouts between guild members are available in Guild Achievements → Shoutouts, with a Give a shoutout form. The GM and rank 1 officers can Delete shoutouts after confirmation; deletion records sync and prevent older history from restoring removed posts. `/gd shout Player Name | message` still works. Achievement cards have a compact Cheers/Thanks/Epic reaction strip with game icons, shared counts, colored hover states, tooltips, and a colored underline for your selected reaction. Each character can choose one reaction, switch choices, or click their selected reaction to remove it. Reactions and withdrawals are saved per guild and synced to updated clients; retained reactions from earlier versions are reused. Reaction controls appear only on achievement cards. Shoutout deletion still requires GM/rank 1 permission; award deletion remains GM-only.
- Raid Info, Guild Info, and Polls show a NEW badge for unseen content. Opening a section marks its current content as read. Read status is saved per character and guild across reloads. Upcoming raid/event creations and edits, Guild Info revisions (including clearing the page), and new or closed polls trigger badges; votes and signup changes do not. Existing content is unread until first opened; expired events and deleted records do not trigger badges.
- Pop-up notifications for new guild announcements and events, stacking PvP rewards, and guild-wide level/profession milestones for members running GuildDB.

Officers (guild leader and rank 1) can create announcements, schedule raids and events, create or close polls, and start PvP seasons. Any guild member can edit their own profile, RSVP, vote, make or claim board requests, and post shoutouts.

Guild data syncs between members running GuildDB using in-game addon messages. There is no external server. Offline members catch up from an online rank 0 or rank 1 member when they sync. Board requests expire automatically.

In Events, officers use Create raid to schedule a raid and set its signup lock and size target. Creation defaults to also creating an in-game guild-calendar signup event; choose GuildDB only to skip it. The creator can retry with Create calendar if the client cannot create the calendar copy. Calendar writes require the client's guild-calendar APIs and game permissions.

Members use Sign up / Change to choose Going, Maybe, or Absent, write their spec, choose Tank/Healer/DPS, and add an optional note. Class is detected automatically from the guild roster; player and class columns use class colors. Role totals count Going players only, with older signups missing a role counted as Unassigned. Members can change an existing signup even when new signups are locked, or use Remove my signup to withdraw. The GM can remove anyone's signup. Removal records are synced to prevent older history from restoring a withdrawn signup; members can sign up again while signups are open.

Only the GM can change the invite checkmarks, and only Going players can be selected. Checkmarks record the planned invite list and do not automatically invite players. All participating members should update GuildDB to exchange the expanded signup data, removals, and GM selections. Raid size is a planning target, so additional members can still sign up. Withdrawing your own signup attempts to decline the matching native calendar event; GM removal affects the GuildDB roster.

GuildDB remembers each character's last reported addon version in the saved data for that guild, including your own characters as you log into them. The roster's Online only filter can be turned off to see offline characters and their saved versions. Member details label an older report as "last known" when there is no recent announcement; this does not prove the addon is still installed. Characters not yet detected show X in the version column. Saved reports do not generate extra messages, and only recent live announcements are used to select sync responders.

GuildDB announces its version and full character name at login and once per minute. Login and guild joins retry discovery during the first 25 seconds; opening GuildDB also requests replies from online peers. Older clients still receive the original version announcement. Use `/gd presence` to inspect prefix registration, outgoing announcements, and incoming guild packets when diagnosing detection problems.

At login or UI reload, GuildDB prints a colored welcome message with `/gd` and your installed version. Use `/gd version` to display your installed version again.

Select a member in Roster and choose a rank from Set Guild Rank. Each hardware click submits one secure rank step and refreshes the roster from the game. If the chosen rank is several steps away, the same control shows Continue to the chosen rank; click again after each confirmed step until it is reached. This avoids sending several commands against the same stale rank. Guild permissions still apply, and rank changes are unavailable during combat. The dropdown stays inside GuildDB and remains open during ordinary background refreshes.

PvP rankings use total eligible kills plus automatic bounty bonuses, regardless of victim level. Each kill contributes one base point; existing kill totals are preserved and historical level-based points no longer affect rankings. Only game-credited open-world kills count; battlegrounds, arenas, dungeons, raids, and scenarios are excluded. Honor messages and kill-credit updates are combined to avoid counting the same reward twice. Counter-only credits require one identifiable recent enemy death. Victim levels come from recently visible enemy players; unknown levels use your level, and skull-level enemies use your level plus 10, capped at 60. Every eligible kill counts, including repeated kills of the same victim, with no per-victim cooldown or daily cap. The detailed activity feed keeps up to 60 recent kills and deaths per guild. Leaderboard kill, point, and death totals are cumulative per member and season, synced separately from that feed. Existing saved recent records are folded into a member's totals the next time they record a PvP event; older records already discarded by the 60-entry cap cannot be recovered. A new season starts fresh at its start time.

## Install for development

Release versions use `0.2.NN`, starting at `0.2.01`. Increment the final number after every five completed addon features or fixes, keeping the TOC version and `GD.version` in sync. Track progress toward the next bump in AGENTS.md; instruction-only and documentation-only updates do not count.

Copy this folder into the client's `Interface/AddOns` directory, enable **GuildDB** on the AddOns screen, and join a guild.

Run once before opening the guild bank, then open a populated tab and run it again. `/gd bankapi query` requests that selected tab's data while the bank is open; wait for the response, then rerun `/gd bankapi`. This diagnostic does not move items, save snapshots, or share bank contents. Runtime results are needed to confirm Forever compatibility.

- `/gd event YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM | Title | Details` schedules an event (officer).
- `/gd raid YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM | Title | Details` schedules a raid (officer).
- `/gd rsvp # going|tentative|absent` responds to an upcoming calendar entry.
- `/gd profile set Role | Spec | Time zone | Alts | Professions` sets your member profile.
- `/gd profile Name` views a member profile; `/gd profile list` returns to the directory.
- `/gd looking Role | Activity | Details` posts a group request; `/gd looking close #` closes your post.
- `/gd craft request Item | Details` posts a crafting request.
- `/gd craft claim #` claims a request; `/gd craft done #` closes it.
- `/gd craft who Recipe` searches online members' listed crafting skills.
- `/gd poll create Question | Option 1 | Option 2 [| Option 3 | Option 4]` creates a poll (officer).
- `/gd poll vote # option#` votes; `/gd poll close #` closes a poll (officer).
- PvP → New Season creates a season (guild master only): enter its name, pick an end date from the calendar, and click Start. Existing seasons and all-time totals are kept. The end date includes that entire day in the creator's local time. When the deadline passes, new kills count toward all-time stats only; the finished season remains visible. All members must update for the cutoff to apply consistently.
- `/gd pvp wipe CONFIRM` permanently clears all guild PvP points, kills, deaths, recent records, and seasons (guild master / rank 0 only). The saved reset syncs to updated members, including members who return later; older clients must update to honor resets. Repeat-victim history clears when a client receives the reset.
- PvP → Delete Season lets the guild master choose a season and confirm its deletion. All-time stats are kept. The button is disabled when no seasons exist.
- PvP → Wipe All Stats lets the guild master clear all PvP stats and seasons after confirmation. Click New Season afterward to begin a new Season 1. These three buttons are grouped at the bottom of the PvP screen.
- `/gd shout Player Name | Message` posts a shoutout.
- `/gd map` explains live World Map pins; `/gd map clear` clears an old WoW waypoint.
- Recruitment defaults to New: a queue of previously found or freshly imported candidates with no recorded whisper or invite. Pending, empty or timed-out Who searches keep untouched candidates visible. Who searches always use exactly the entered level or range; changing it immediately filters saved candidates to those levels too. Who results admit only unguilded players, and saved candidates are hidden if newer results show they joined a guild or their guild status is unavailable. Search summaries show unguilded and uncontacted counts. Contact history does not consume the 500-place New queue, and import capacity failures are shown explicitly. Recruitment candidate names use class colors, and contacted statuses stand out in green (replies in blue, Do Not Contact in red). The popout's Contacted dropdown option and the full window's Contacted filter include everyone previously whispered or invited, even after a reply or status change. This private contact history is independent of the current Who search. The recruiting popout can shrink to 320 x 440 while keeping its controls and replies visible.
- `/gd popout pvp`, `/gd popout level10` (also 20/30/40/50/60), or `/gd popout profession150` (also 225/300) previews a popout locally.
- `/gd testpopout level` or `/gd testpopout profession` sends a milestone popup test to online GuildDB users (guild leader/rank 1).

Personal Settings can turn GuildDB popups on or off, optionally share your map location with GuildDB members, and optionally send a guild-chat “Ding!” for level milestones. Map sharing is off by default, pauses in instances, and reports expire after 45 seconds. Profession milestones never send guild chat messages.

Date and time in event commands use the local time shown by the game client. Board item numbers refer to the current order shown in their tab.

## DKP workflow

All participating clients need the updated addon and `/reload`. Enable DKP in Settings > Officer. Saved data is separated by guild; disabling DKP preserves it. Use one auction host at a time, and let that host confirm item awards.

### Prices and starting points

Prices / Rules displays the guild's starting prices and lets members look up equipment by link or numeric ID. Officers can save individual item overrides; a saved override takes priority over its slot price. Unknown or uncached equipment is left unpriced until item data loads or an officer enters a price.

| Gear | Starting DKP price |
| --- | ---: |
| Neck / rings | 100 |
| Cloak / wrists / waist / feet / wand / thrown | 150 |
| Head / chest / shoulders / legs / hands | 200 |
| One-hand weapons / shields / off-hands | 250 |
| Two-hand / ranged weapons | 350 |
| Trinkets | 300 |

Officer > Starting DKP opens an unchecked roster of eligible level-60 characters. Select the raiders, review, then grant 1,000 DKP once per linked account. Existing points are preserved. Stable grant IDs prevent repeated grants through retries, reloads and history sync. Link alts to mains before granting points. If both characters already received starting grants, reverse the extra grant before linking.

Standings includes offline level-60 characters, class colors, search and DKP sorting. Hover a member to see the shared main/alt account. Award and Take DKP require whole numbers and a reason; these manual adjustments can create negative balances. Auctions and normal loot purchases require sufficient available funds.

### Auction and loot windows

1. Join a raid, then open DKP > Auctions > Start Auction.
2. Shift-click/paste an item link or enter its item ID. Alternatively, with DKP enabled, the GM or a rank-1 officer can Alt + right-click a bag item, a loot-window item or an item link in chat to open its auction setup directly. Review the prefilled slot/item price, minimum increase and duration, then press Start to publish the auction to eligible level-60 guild raiders. If an auction is already active, the shortcut reopens it instead of creating another.
3. Players receive a movable, resizable, themed popup with an item tooltip, countdown bar (red for the final ten seconds), highlighted leader and available shared DKP. Quick Bid uses the next valid bid for the selected MS/OS pool; minus/plus adjust it by the auction's increment. Custom Bid submits the typed amount, while All In submits the available shared balance when it meets the next bid and maximum bid limit. The scrollable table shows every bidder, their spec and amount, class-colored names, and your own bid. Accepted bids synchronize through the hosting officer. A player can withdraw while bidding is open. Players can disable automatic popups and reopen an auction through Bid Window in the Auctions tab, which also shows the live countdown and bidder list.
4. Main-spec bids have priority over off-spec bids. Each spec's new bid must exceed that spec's leading bid by the configured increase. Acceptance order breaks equal-price ties. Bids accepted in the final 10 seconds extend the deadline to allow a response.
5. The host closes bidding or lets the timer close it. Confirm winner checks current raid eligibility and funds again, records the item/spec/cost and deducts DKP exactly once. An unaffordable or ineligible winner's bid can be removed before reviewing again.
6. The loot master distributes the actual game item. The addon records points and history; it does not call protected loot-distribution actions.

Loot > Record Loot supports a reviewed purchase without an auction. Loot history shows the recipient, price, spec, officer and time. Cancelled auctions spend no DKP. If another host disconnects, officers can use Stop Stale 60 seconds after the bidding deadline; finished purchases are corrected through a reversal.

### Raid and attendance recording

Auction comfort controls: the popup remembers its resized width/height in personal settings, shows your own leading/outbid status beside available DKP, and disables Withdraw until you have a bid. Enter submits the custom amount while bidding is open; Escape releases the amount field's focus. The countdown bar interpolates between seconds while the auction host's deadline remains authoritative.

Auctions > Queue lets officers add up to 40 drops with reviewed minimum bids, increments and durations, reorder them or remove an item. Each character's queue is saved locally for that guild and survives reloads; it is not included in ledger backups or shared with other officers. Start Next opens the first queued item only after the current auction finishes. Failed starts preserve the queued item. Repeated copies of the same item are allowed.

The queue opens a themed, movable list with item icons, rarity-colored names, hover tooltips, starting bids, increments and timers. Up / Down changes the order; Remove drops an entry. Add item and Start next remain at the bottom while the list scrolls.

Loot awards begin as Pending. In Loot, officers can Mark delivered after distributing the item, or Mark pending to undo that confirmation. Players can see the status; only the GM and rank-1 officers can change it. Delivery changes synchronize, persist through reloads and backups, and appear in raid summaries without charging DKP again. Refunded awards show Refunded and cannot be marked delivered. Delivery is a manual confirmation, not automatic trade detection.

Officer > Sync Status checks the current DKP snapshot against replies from updated guild clients. The report shows Confirmed, Different data, Waiting for reply, No response, or Offline. Confirmation applies to the snapshot at the check time; later local changes prompt another check. Check Again sends a new request, with a five-second cooldown. Offline members and clients that do not reply are never assumed synchronized. Sync reports are temporary and are not included in backups. Exit Solo Test before checking guild sync.

History has an All / Earned / Spent / Loot / Corrections filter that combines with its text search. Earned and Spent show positive and negative original entries; Loot includes purchases and their refunds; Corrections includes manual deductions and reversals. Original entries remain visible in their category after reversal, with their compensating entries preserved in the audit trail.

Auctions > Alerts contains personal popup, outbid sound and screen-border flash preferences. Sound and flash default off. An alert fires once when another bidder takes your lead, including while your popup is dismissed. Opening history, joining an auction already in progress, withdrawing your bid, or closing bidding does not produce an outbid alert.

Raids > Summary opens a scrollable report with attendance checkpoints per character, bosses killed, total and individual DKP earned, and loot awards with refund markers. Checkpoints opens the original attendee lists; Refresh updates a running raid's summary. New auction awards and manual purchases are linked to the raid active when the purchase is recorded, and these links synchronize and survive backup/restore. Earlier purchases without a saved raid link are not guessed into a summary. All participating clients should update to display these new links.

For solo testing, use `/gd dkptest` or DKP > Raids > Solo Test as the GM or a rank-1 officer. This opens a temporary local DKP session with 1,000 points, your character as the participant, and no raid or level requirement. You can record raids, award bosses, auction items, bid, confirm winners and inspect loot by yourself. SOLO TEST appears in the DKP headings. Test data is never broadcast or saved into guild DKP; live DKP messages are held until you exit. Use `/gd dkptest` again or Exit Test to discard the session and return to guild data. Reloading also discards the test session. Backups and restores require exiting Solo Test first.

Raids > Start Raid begins a recording inside the instance and awards 10 on-time DKP to connected guild raiders currently present, once per linked account. During testing, raid recording and its attendance/boss rewards accept guild members of any level; the standings and auction eligibility still require level 60. The recording host handles supported ENCOUNTER_END/BOSS_KILL notifications. Repeated notifications for the same boss in the same recording are ignored. A boss kill grants 20 DKP; the guild's first recorded kill in that zone also grants 10 bonus DKP. Replayed or out-of-order kill history reconciles that first bonus to one recorded kill.

Boss kills award DKP automatically during a recorded raid. Use Award Raid for reviewed custom adjustments when needed. End Raid awards 10 DKP to eligible connected guild raiders currently present and finishes recording. Stop Recording preserves earned points and stops without an attendance bonus, including after the host disconnects or the group disbands. Attendance opens each checkpoint's actual recipient list. Ordinary boss/attendance presets are blocked during an active recording to avoid accidentally awarding the same event through both paths; custom adjustments remain available.

While a raid recording is active, the Raids view shows that automatic boss awards are running and identifies the recording host. A successful encounter or boss-kill notification records the award automatically and prints the boss, DKP amount and recipient count for that host. Wipes and repeated notifications do not create awards; offline, departed and non-guild players are excluded from the current kill's recipient list. The auction popup uses a layer above the main addon window, with review and confirmation forms above the popup.

### Officer corrections and recovery

Officer contains the searchable audit ledger, main/alt linking, starting grants and backup controls. Linking shares saved balances and future spending without rewriting historical authors or recipients. Links cannot change during active auctions. Select an original ledger entry, enter a reason and confirm a reversal; the original stays visible and one opposite adjustment is added. A duplicate reversal is ignored. Loot refunds and starting-grant corrections use the same audit trail.

Backup exports copyable text. Save it outside the game. Restore validates the current guild header and all records, merges into staged data, and commits only when replay succeeds. It preserves existing entries and applies duplicate records once; invalid input or a replay error leaves original data intact. Successful restores synchronize the merged records through authorized officers. No Lua from a backup is executed.

Run `node Tests/DKP.test.js` when those packages are available to Node.
